Room Assignments

Set or change the actual school room/venue hosting each cluster (e.g. Cluster A3 → "1K1", Cluster D4 → "Senior Corridor") — this becomes the "actual room on the ground" shown to mentors and students, alongside the cluster code. Everyone signed in can see these; Leads, Assistant Leads, Zone Coordinators, and Interns can edit them — room coordination is exactly the kind of thing that gets delegated to an intern.

Room Map & Coordination

A link to the campus/room map, and who's in charge of room coordination and mapping — shown to everyone in Schedule → My Room. Paste a link to the map image once it's generated and uploaded (e.g. a Google Drive/Photos share link, or a path on the app's own site).

Classes & Streams

The actual class/stream names students pick from when registering (e.g. "4 East", "10 Amani") — grouped under Form 4, Grade 10 — Group A, or Grade 10 — Group B, which is how sessions are grouped regardless of the class name itself. No delete from here — fix a typo with Save, or edit the Sheet directly to remove one.

Session Schedule (Time Slots)

The clock time each cohort's Round 1–4 runs — shown to students (Find Student/My Class) and in Schedule → My Room, so everyone knows where to be at, say, 10:30am. Leads, Assistant Leads, Zone Coordinators, and Interns can all edit this, to keep it in sync as the wider Career Day programme firms up.
