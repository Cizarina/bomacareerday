What This Is
Boma Career Day 2026 is the Kenya High School Alumnae Society's mentorship & exhibition day for Form 4 and Grade 10 students — mentors from 23 career clusters run small-group sessions, alongside lab visits and an exhibition tour. WG2 is the working group running mentorship & logistics for the day. This app is WG2's shared system for registration, check-in, scheduling, team coordination, and now this Docs tab. It's built for the WG2 team, not for students or the general public.

This tab is visible to the core WG2 team only — Leads, Assistant Leads, Zone Coordinators, Cluster Leads/Sub-Leads, Interns and Class Teachers. Mentors don't see it.

Quick answers to the most common questions. Still stuck? Use Feedback / Report a Problem above, or message your Lead.

How do I install the app on my phone?

iPhone / iPad (Safari): open the app link in Safari → tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) → Add to Home Screen → tap Add.

Android (Chrome): open the app link in Chrome → tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen (or Install app) → confirm.

Laptop/desktop (Chrome or Edge): open the link → click the install icon in the address bar (or the ⋮ menu → Install BOMA Career Day - CMP Mentors Hub).

Installing isn't required — the app works fine in a browser tab too. Installing just gives you a home-screen icon and a full-screen, app-like feel.

Where do I find my session time and room?

Sign in, then go to the Schedule tab and tap the My Room chip — it auto-detects your cluster and shows the room plus the time and cohort for each round you're mentoring.

Mentors and Interns also get a My Day view on the Dashboard tab with the same info at a glance, and a "Your Session Guide" button.

Class Teachers: use the My Class chip on the Schedule tab instead.

How do I scan a student's or mentor's QR code?

Go to the Check-In tab (Scan QR is the default chip) → tap Start Camera → point your camera at their QR code. It checks them in automatically once it reads.

Code not scanning, or no code at all? Use the Search chip to find them by name, or Walk-in to register and check them in on the spot.

How do I change my PIN?

Tap your name at the top of the screen to open your Account panel, enter a new 4–6 digit PIN under My PIN, and confirm. Your new PIN is shown on screen right away.

The email that sent you your PIN also has a Change My PIN button that jumps straight to this screen.

The app looks stuck, blank, or out of date — what do I do?

Fully close the app and reopen it — twice. It updates itself automatically in the background, and a second reopen picks up the refreshed version.

Still stuck? Check you're signed in (tap Sign In at the top with your full name + PIN), and make sure you have a working internet connection.

If it's still wrong, use Feedback / Report a Problem above and tell us what screen you were on.
