Meet the App: Boma Career Day — WG2 Hub
One app for everything the team needs to run Career Day. Here's what it does for you.

How to Use It

1
Open the app

cizarina.github.io/bomacareerday — bookmark it or add it to your home screen for one-tap access.

2
Sign in

Full name + your 4-digit PIN. No PIN yet? Ask a Lead or Assistant Lead to set one for you.

3
Find your section

Everything you need is one tap from the home screen — see the screens below.

4
Stuck or something looks broken?

Fully close and reopen the app twice. It updates itself automatically in the background.

What you're actually being asked to do
Split by who's reading this — skip to your part.

🧑‍💼 For Interns

  • You can bulk-import mentor sign-ups collected outside the app (spreadsheet, WhatsApp, paper) straight into the system: Dashboard → Mentor Applications → Bulk Import Mentors. Paste a table or upload the file, and every mentor is created and confirmed in one go.
  • You have access to the Leads & Interns chat channel in-app (tap the chat icon) — use it for anything you need routed quickly.

🤝 For the Rest of the Team (Mentors, Cluster & Zone Coordinators, everyone else)

  • Share the app's registration links with anyone who wants to sign up — Mentor Registration and Parent/KHS Student Registration are both big buttons right on the landing page.
  • Check-in on the day: Cluster Sub-Leads scan each student's/mentor's QR code at the room door.
  • Room E3 (The Arts) covers a wide range of careers, including Film and Music industry roles alongside the original design/performing arts offerings — worth knowing if you're near that zone.
  • Use the in-app Careers & Clusters Guide to answer "which room is X in?" on the spot — it's searchable, and both PDFs are downloadable from the same screen if you'd rather carry a printed copy.
